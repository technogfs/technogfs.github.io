## Human-Friendly Brand Book

### Business Vision:
TechnoGFS is a company that creates food colors with proper licensing for European distribution standards. The company is now looking to expand its reach by creating multi-lingual websites that introduce their wide range of products for different foods. The product can be customized based on the application of the coloring like beverages, ice-cream, confectionary, and fat-based products. The formulation can also be customized per customer order for higher density or special color range. The support is also considerable. It includes stability reviews, dosage guidance, scale-up feedback and so on.

### Strategy:
1. Develop multi-lingual websites to reach a broader audience in Europe.
2. Offer customized food color products based on customer needs and application requirements.
3. Provide comprehensive support services including technical reviews, application samples, and commercial delivery.

### Brand Voice:
Professional, innovative, and customer-centric. The brand voice should reflect the company's commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

### Color Palette:
Use warm colors such as red, orange, yellow, and brown to reflect the food industry and evoke feelings of warmth and appetite.

### Fonts:
Use clean, modern fonts that are easy to read and reflect professionalism. Sans-serif fonts like Arial or Helvetica are recommended.

### Branding and Positioning:
The brand should be positioned as a leader in the food color industry, known for its quality products, customization options, and comprehensive support services.
